Our philosophy

We believe childhood should be filled with laughter, friendship and meaningful experiences. Children learn best through play, exploration and discovery — and our fantastic team inspire curiosity, creativity and a lifelong love of learning.

We celebrate diversity and foster a strong sense of belonging, working hand-in-hand with families to support each child's growth, development and happiness.

Our curriculum

Our programme is guided by Te Whāriki, the New Zealand early childhood curriculum, and built around play-based learning that grows the whole child.

  • Creative arts — painting, drawing & crafts
  • Early literacy — storytime, phonics & language
  • Math & science through hands-on exploration
  • Music, movement & physical development
  • Safe, supervised outdoor play
