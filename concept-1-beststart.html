Our story

Every business has a beginning, but ours started with something deeper: a desire to create meaningful impact. Before starting Happy Horizons, I spent many years working in financial services — a career built on numbers and systems that left me searching for something more fulfilling.

Conversations with my cousin Bernadette, who has spent over 20 years in early childhood education, sparked a new calling: helping shape children's earliest experiences — their confidence, their sense of belonging and their love of learning.

— Francis Wolfgram, Centre Owner
